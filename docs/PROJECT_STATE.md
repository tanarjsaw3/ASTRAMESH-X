## 3. Implemented but Not Yet Verified / Known Limitations

- **Host Virtualization / WSL2 Unavailability:** Probed WSL2 environment returned `Wsl/Service/E_UNEXPECTED` due to Windows hypervisor service failure on the host machine. Handled per Sprint Rule 10: native MSYS2 UCRT64 GCC was preserved for all 24 C++ targets, and complete ROS 2 Jazzy and Gazebo Harmonic architectural blueprints, package layouts, and migration guides were delivered in `docs/ROS2_GAZEBO_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md`.
- **Incomplete SC10 Swarm 70-Cell Sweep:** To prevent a 13-hour overnight laptop run, the 8-rover swarm benchmark was halted cleanly at 680 of 2,450 trials (27.8% across 20 of 70 cells). These records are preserved and analyzed strictly as exploratory scaling evidence, and must not be cited as a complete 70-cell factorial sweep.
- **`NoACKs` Ablation Conflation:** Disabling ACKs in `pipeline_astromesh_x.cpp` eliminates explicit 44 B ACK packets and simultaneously eliminates the sender's ACK-awaiting state. Under high latency ($\tau \ge 50\,\text{ms}$), `NoACKs` never triggers timeout-based retransmissions, obscuring the boundary between ACK frame overhead and latency-induced duplicate suppression.
- **Fixed Retransmission Timeout (RTO):** The static 50 ms initial timeout causes step-function spurious retransmissions when $RTT = 2\tau > 50\,\text{ms}$, scaling total wire bytes up to $5\times$ at $500\,\text{ms}$ latency. Adaptive RTT estimation (Jacobson/Karn) is needed for arbitrary delay environments.
- **Synthetic Network vs. Physical Radio:** The simulation uses stochastic discrete-event scheduling (latency, jitter, packet loss, partitions) and exact wire byte accounting (payload + 24B header + 20B transport overhead), but does not model physical RF attenuation, antenna radiation patterns, or multi-path fading.
- **Factor-Removal Ablation vs. Marginalization:** Deleting pose-graph nodes and incident edges without computing Schur-complement dense marginals discards measurement constraints. Benchmarks measure memory reduction and topological safety invariants, and are explicitly characterized as factor-removal ablations rather than information-preserving graph sparsification.
- **Multi-Seed Transient Cost Variations Under Delay:** While aligned ATE is consistently preserved ($< 0.12\,\mathrm{m}$ across all seeds), transient cost increases can occur at intermediate delays (50 ms) for specific initial noise seeds. Adaptive damping is an empirical stabilizing heuristic, not an unconditional Lyapunov convergence proof.
- **AstroMesh v1 Baseline Trajectory Drift:** As intended by design, `BaselineAstroMeshV1` performs dead-reckoning open-loop integration without trajectory smoothing or optimization; ATE will accumulate monotonically along odometry paths and will not be corrected by inter-rover loop closures.
